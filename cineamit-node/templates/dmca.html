DMCA / Copyright Policy

CINEAMIT DOES NOT HOST ANY FILES ON ITS SERVERS.

CINEAMIT operates as an index and database of movie and TV show links found publicly on the Internet, much like a search engine. We do not host, upload, encode, store or control any of the videos, media files, or content streamed through our platform.

All video content is hosted by non-affiliated third-party content providers (such as Google Drive, Vidsrc, YouTube, Doodstream, and similar services). Therefore, CINEAMIT does not have direct control over the media embedded through those hosts.

Filing a DMCA Takedown Notice

If you are a copyright owner (or authorized to act on their behalf) and believe that content indexed on our site infringes your copyright, you may send us a DMCA takedown request. It must include all of the following:

  1. The physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner, or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed (title, and where possible the specific version/episode).
  3. The exact URL(s) on CINEAMIT where the infringing link is located (page link, not just the video file URL).
  4. Your contact information — full name, email address, and (for organizations) title/role.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the work in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the copyright owner's behalf, and that the information in the notice is accurate.

Submit your DMCA request to legal@cineamit.com or through the Contact page (select "DMCA / Copyright" as the subject).

Our Response Process

  • Valid, properly-formed notices are acknowledged and processed within 7 days of receipt.
  • Matching links are removed from our index, and repeat links to the same infringing file are blocked from being re-indexed.
  • In most cases, removing the file at the third-party host (e.g. the Drive owner) also removes it from CINEAMIT automatically, since our system relies on their links. We recommend contacting the host directly for fastest results.

Counter-Notice

If your content was removed in error (for example, you own the content or have a license), you may send a counter-notice to the same address that includes:

  • Your signature, contact information, and the URL of the removed item.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the item was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  • Your name, address, and a consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court in your district (or, if outside the U.S., your local courts).

Upon receipt of a valid counter-notice we may reinstate the link, provided the complainant does not file a court action within a reasonable period.

Repeat Infringer Policy

We respect intellectual property rights. Users of CINEAMIT who are found to be repeat abusers — for example, repeatedly uploading or promoting infringing links through community features — will have their accounts suspended or terminated.
