What CINEAMIT Is — and Isn't

What We Are

  • A search engine and index for publicly available video links — we point you to content, like a directory.
  • A fast, clean streaming interface with multiple server options so playback keeps moving.
  • A community platform — watch parties, requests, reviews, watchlists and profiles.
  • A free Live TV guide with channel listings and EPG information.

What We Are Not

  • We do not host any movies, shows or files on our own servers. All media is hosted by third parties.
  • We do not own any of the films or shows we index. All copyrights remain with their respective owners.
