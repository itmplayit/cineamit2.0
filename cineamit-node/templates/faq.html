Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about CINEAMIT — accounts, playback, Watch Party, Live TV and more.

Getting Started

Is CINEAMIT completely free?
Yes — CINEAMIT is 100% free. No credit card, no subscription, no hidden fees, no premium tier. You can watch unlimited movies, TV shows and Live TV without paying anything.
Do I need to create an account to watch?
No — you can start watching immediately without registering. A free account unlocks: watch history, watchlists, custom playlists, collections, content requests, reviews and Watch Party chat.
How do I find a specific movie or show?
Use the search bar at the top of any page and type the title (it works for movies, shows and even actors). You can also browse by category — Popular, Top Rated, Trending, Genres, and dedicated sections for Bollywood, South Indian (Hindi dubbed), Anime and Live TV. The "Top 10" row on the home page shows what's trending right now.

Account & Security

I forgot my password — what do I do?
On the login page click "Forgot Password?" and enter your registered email. We send a one-time 6-digit verification code (valid for 10 minutes). Enter the code and set a new password. If you don't receive the email, check spam and make sure you typed the same email you registered with.
What is 2FA and should I enable it?
Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) adds a second step to login: after your password, you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator). Go to Profile → Privacy & Security → Enable 2FA, add the secret to your app, and confirm with the code. We strongly recommend it — it's the single best way to keep your account safe. Admin accounts are required to have 2FA enabled.
Can I have multiple profiles? Is there a Kids mode?
Yes. After logging in, go to the Profiles page and add profiles for family members — each with its own avatar, theme, watch history and PIN lock. The built-in Kids profile filters for family/children genres and checks TMDB age certifications before opening details or playback; titles with missing or unsupported ratings are blocked. Rating data can still be incomplete, so please supervise children.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm with your password. This permanently removes your account and everything attached to it — history, watchlists, playlists, reviews and linked social logins. This action cannot be undone.

Watching & Playback

Why is my video buffering or not playing?
If a video is buffering or fails to load, that particular server may be under heavy load. On the player page, look for the "Select Server" buttons just above the video — try Server 2 or Server 3. If all servers fail for one title, the source links may be down; try again later or pick an alternative title. A slow internet connection can also cause buffering — check your speed or try a lower quality option if available.
What quality does CINEAMIT play in?
Quality depends on the source file and the server you're using — from 480p up to 1080p (FHD) for most popular titles. You can switch servers to find a better-quality stream, and some players show a quality menu inside the player controls.
Can I watch on my phone, tablet or Smart TV?
Yes — CINEAMIT is fully responsive. On phones and tablets, just open the site in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet, etc.). On Smart TVs, use the TV's built-in browser, or cast from your phone using Chrome Cast / AirPlay. For the best TV experience, open the site on a laptop and cast, since some TV browsers are limited.
Which browsers are supported?
All modern browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari and Samsung Internet. Keep your browser up to date — very old versions may fail to play some streams. If a stream is blocked by a browser extension (adblockers sometimes over-block players), try a private window or temporarily disabling the extension for our domain.
Is there an Android app?
Yes — the official CINEAMIT Android app is on cineamit.qzz.io/app. Download the APK, allow install from Chrome, then open. Live TV, offline downloads and ad-points premium run on your phone. Only install from this official page.
Are there download links?
For some titles, a download section is shown on the detail page with direct links provided by third parties. Availability varies by title and changes over time. Downloaded content is your responsibility — make sure you have the right to save that content in your region.

Watch Party

How does the Watch Party feature work?
When you open a movie or episode, you'll see "Join" and "Invite" buttons at the top. Click "Invite" to copy a unique room link and send it to your friends. When they open it, everyone lands in the same room — you watch together with a synced stream and a real-time chat panel.
My friends can't join the Watch Party — what should we check?
Check three things: (1) the room link is complete — it must be copied in full; (2) your friends are using a modern browser without an aggressive adblocker; (3) the room hasn't been closed by the host. If it still fails, create a fresh room and share the new link.

Live TV

How do I use the Live TV section?
Open the Live TV page from the main menu. Channels are grouped by category (News, Entertainment, Sports, Kids, Music). Click any channel to start the live stream, and use the EPG guide to see what's on now and next. You can pin your favorite channels to the top for quick access.
A Live TV channel is black-screen or not loading — what do I do?
First, try refreshing the channel — live streams sometimes restart after a few seconds. If it persists, the source for that channel may be down (happens with free streams); try another channel from the same group, or check back in a few hours. You can also report broken channels through the Contact page so we can rotate the sources.

Content & Requests

Can I request a movie or TV show?
Absolutely. Log in, go to your Profile page and use the "Submit Request" section — enter the title (movie/show/Live channel) and any details. Our admins review requests regularly and add the most-requested titles first. You can also request via the Contact page.
Why can't I find a specific movie on the site?
Our catalog is built from publicly available sources, so brand-new releases and very niche titles can take time to appear (or may not appear at all). Try searching with alternate names (Hindi dubbed titles often have different names), check the genre pages, or submit a request — if enough people ask, it gets priority.

Legal & Safety

Is CINEAMIT safe to use?
The platform itself is safe: you can watch in the browser with no install. The official Android APK is only at /app — ignore any other “CineAmit APK” site. Use a modern browser with an adblocker, never enter your password off this domain, and enable 2FA. Streams are provided by third parties, so quality can vary.
Does CINEAMIT host or own the movies?
No. CINEAMIT is an index and interface — like a directory — and does not host any video files. All media is hosted by third-party services, and all copyrights remain with their respective owners. If you're a rights holder, see our DMCA Policy. For full details read the Legal Notices, Terms and Privacy Policy.
