Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Short Version

CINEAMIT is a free streaming index. We collect the minimum data needed to run your account and improve the service, we never sell your personal information to anyone, and you can delete your account — and all data attached to it — at any time from your profile.

Android ad reliability diagnostics

When enabled by the operator, signed-in non-kids Android clients send limited operational ad reports: SDK load/show result codes, app and SDK versions, public placement identifiers, shared configuration fingerprint and test-mode flag. Reports are associated with your account for troubleshooting and retained for about seven days, subject to cleanup. They do not contain advertising/device IDs, raw SDK error messages, page URLs or reward tokens. Client reports cannot authorize points; only server-verified reward callbacks can. Ads configuration changes are logged with the administrator and old/new settings for up to 90 days; this audit log is retained separately from ordinary account data for security.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in two categories:

  • Information you provide: If you create an account, we store your username, email address, and an encrypted (hashed) version of your password. We never store your password in plain text. Optional profile fields (avatar, themes, PIN, notes) are stored only if you fill them in.
  • Information generated automatically: Your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages you visit. This helps us route traffic, prevent abuse, and understand what the community watches.
  • Your activity data: Watch history, watchlists, collections, playlists, reviews and Watch Party rooms — all tied to your account so they follow you across devices.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Provide and personalize your experience (watchlists, history, recommendations, profiles).
  • Maintain and synchronize your Watch Parties and Live TV sessions.
  • Improve website performance, server routing and content discovery.
  • Prevent abuse, spam, and technical issues (rate limiting, security logs).
  • Respond to support requests and content requests.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

We use essential session cookies to keep you logged in and save UI preferences (theme color, autoplay settings, active profile). We may use browser local storage for playback preferences. If Google Analytics or an advertising partner is enabled by the site operator, that provider may set or read cookies or similar identifiers; the provider and its own privacy policy then apply. You can block cookies in your browser, though sign-in and saved preferences may stop working.

5. Third Parties

  • TMDb (The Movie Database): Movie titles, posters, backdrops and metadata are provided via the TMDb API, under their terms. CINEAMIT is not endorsed or certified by TMDb.
  • Social login (Google / GitHub): If you sign in with a social account, that provider receives a standard OAuth request. We receive your public profile email and name — nothing more.
  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics 4 is optional and is not loaded unless the operator explicitly configures a measurement ID. If enabled, Google may receive technical usage data such as page views, device/browser details and network identifiers under Google's privacy terms.
  • Advertising partners: If advertising is enabled and provider tags are configured, providers such as Adsterra, HilltopAds or Google AdSense may receive request/device data and may use cookies or similar identifiers. The active providers depend on the operator's configuration.
  • Video hosts: Actual video files are embedded from third-party servers. Those services have their own privacy policies, which apply when you stream through them.

CINEAMIT acts as a search engine and directory. We do not host media files on our servers and we have no control over third-party sites.

6. Data Security

We protect your account with industry-standard practices: passwords are hashed with a strong one-way algorithm; all connections should be made over HTTPS; access to personal data is restricted to authorized staff; login attempts are rate-limited; and you can enable two-factor authentication (2FA) in your profile for an extra layer of protection. No method of transmission is 100% secure, so we also encourage you to keep your own devices and passwords safe.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your account data is retained while your account exists. You can delete your account permanently from Profile → Delete Account, which removes your user record and all related data (history, watchlists, playlists, reviews, notifications, linked social logins). Deleted data is not recovered. Log files and basic security events are retained for a limited period for abuse prevention and then purged automatically.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: Your profile page shows the data stored for you.
  • Correction: Update your username, email, password and preferences anytime from your profile.
  • Deletion: One-click account deletion as described above.
  • Questions: Contact us through the Contact page and we will respond.

9. Children's Privacy

CINEAMIT is a general-audience platform and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. The built-in Kids profile combines family/children genres with a conservative TMDB certification whitelist on detail and playback routes; missing or unsupported ratings are blocked. Provider metadata may be incomplete, so this is not a substitute for parental supervision.

10. International Users

We serve users worldwide. By using CINEAMIT, you acknowledge that your data may be processed on servers located in countries other than your own, and you consent to such processing in accordance with this policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, reach us via the Contact page or by submitting a support request in your profile. We aim to respond to all privacy-related requests promptly.
